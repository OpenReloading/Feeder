# Assembly

Each step moves its parts in first, then their screws, then their nuts. Drag to orbit, hold Ctrl or Cmd and scroll to zoom (on a phone, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan, and the Orbit mode button for one-finger orbiting). [Open the viewer on its own page](generated/assembly/viewer.html). The assembled feeder as [STEP](generated/assembly/feeder.step) (labelled part tree, FreeCAD and Fusion) and [3MF](generated/assembly/feeder.3mf).
