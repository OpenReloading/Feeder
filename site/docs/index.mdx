## What it is, and who it is for

<div className="feeder-cards">
<div className="feeder-card">
<p className="feeder-card__label">WHAT IT IS</p>

Brass goes into the bowl in any orientation, up to 250 9mm cases. An inductive sensor stops the motor when the tube is full.

</div>
<div className="feeder-card">
<p className="feeder-card__label">WHO IT IS FOR</p>

For reloaders who 3D print their own tools and can buy a gear motor, an M18 inductive sensor and metric DIN/ISO hardware. The default feeder holds up to 250 9mm cases and prints on a 256 mm bed.

</div>
<div className="feeder-card">
<p className="feeder-card__label">HOW IT WORKS</p>

The plate pockets take base-down cases. Mouth-down cases sit high and fall back at the tilt. Cases leave base down (mouth up) through the drop tube.

</div>
</div>

Every printed part prints without supports, except a few small support islands on the body core, the drop tube and the clamp. Print orientation STLs come from build.py (`build/print/`).
