# Assembly

Each step moves its parts in first, then their screws, then their nuts. Drag to orbit, hold Ctrl or Cmd and scroll to zoom (on a phone, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan, and the Orbit mode button for one-finger orbiting). The assembled feeder comes as STEP (labelled part tree, FreeCAD and Fusion) and 3MF.
